Export video

About voice in exported video: browsers do not let a web page record its own text-to-speech output. To get the voice into the file, pick “Capture everything”: your browser will ask what to share — choose Entire screen and tick Share system audio (Windows/ChromeOS Chrome or Edge). Tab-only sharing usually skips the speech voice. Keep this tab visible while recording.
